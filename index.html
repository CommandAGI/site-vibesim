Four verbs, one run

  1. 01describeOne JSON object: the machine, where each joint starts, the point to reach, the load.
  2. 02instantiateThe SO-101 is built from its published joint ranges. Each arc is a joint’s whole range.
  3. 03simulateInverse kinematics finds the angles; each servo is stepped at one kilohertz until the arm settles.
  4. 04inspectEvery joint’s angle over time, inside its band. A trace at the band’s edge is a joint at its limit.

What the description says

    

      
machine
Which arm, and where every joint starts. The bench models the SO-101’s published joint ranges and servo limits.

      
goal_mm
The point the tool must reach, in millimetres. Inverse kinematics finds angles that get there, inside their ranges.

      
payload_g
What the gripper holds, in grams. Gravity and the load show up as torque on every servo in the chain.
